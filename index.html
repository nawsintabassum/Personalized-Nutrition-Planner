Dietary Guidelines & Meal Schedule

Specific dietary recommendations depending on whether your goal is gaining healthy weight or shedding body fat.

Weight Loss Plan

How to Lose Fat Effectively

Focus on a controlled caloric deficit with high satiety foods.

⏰ Recommended Meal Timing

  • 07:30 AM - Breakfast: High-protein meal (Eggs, Oats, Black Coffee) to keep you full.
  • 01:00 PM - Lunch: Lean protein (Chicken/Fish) + large bowl of green salad + minimal complex carbs (Brown rice/Quinoa).
  • 05:00 PM - Evening Snack: Green tea with a handful of almonds or cucumber slices.
  • 08:00 PM - Dinner: Light meal eaten at least 2 hours before sleeping. Avoid heavy carbs at night.

💡 Key Rules

  • Drink at least 3-4 liters of water daily.
  • Completely eliminate refined sugar, fast food, and sodas.
  • Prioritize fiber and protein to preserve muscle mass during fat loss.
Weight Gain Plan

How to Build Healthy Weight & Muscle

Focus on nutrient-dense calorie surplus without eating empty junk calories.

⏰ Recommended Meal Timing

  • 08:00 AM - Breakfast: Whole eggs, whole-wheat toast, peanut butter, and fruit smoothies.
  • 11:00 AM - Mid-Morning Snack: Handful of mixed nuts (Walnuts, Cashews, Raisins) or a banana shake.
  • 01:30 PM - Lunch: Generous portions of Rice/Pasta, Lean Meat, Milk, and Cooked Vegetables in Olive Oil.
  • 05:30 PM - Pre-Workout Snack: Peanut butter sandwich or boiled potatoes.
  • 08:30 PM - Dinner: Dense protein meal with healthy fats (Salmon, Cottage cheese, Whole Grains).

💡 Key Rules

  • Eat frequently (5-6 smaller nutrient-dense meals a day).
  • Drink liquid calories like homemade protein smoothies with milk, bananas, and oats.
  • Ensure 7-8 hours of quality sleep for muscle recovery and growth.

Exercise & Training Guidelines

Pairing your diet with the correct training program ensures optimal body composition results.

🏋️ Training for Weight / Fat Loss

Combine cardiovascular conditioning with resistance training to maximize calorie burn and preserve lean muscle.

Cardio & HIIT (3x / week)

20-30 minutes of High-Intensity Interval Training (Brisk walking, Jump rope, Cycling, or Treadmill sprints).

Full Body Strength Training (3x / week)

Squats, Push-ups, Dumbbell Presses, and Planks. Lifting weights prevents muscle loss while losing weight.

Daily Steps Goal

Aim for 8,000 to 10,000 steps daily to increase Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT).

💪 Training for Muscle & Weight Gain

Focus strictly on progressive overload resistance training to convert surplus calories into lean muscle rather than fat.

Progressive Resistance Training (4-5x / week)

Focus on heavy compound movements: Barbell Squats, Deadlifts, Bench Press, Overhead Press, and Pull-ups.

Minimize Excessive Cardio

Keep cardio light (10-15 minutes warm-up) so you do not burn off the excess calories needed for building weight.

Rest & Recovery

Muscle grows during rest, not during workouts. Allow 48 hours of rest before retraining the same muscle group.
